# Decision — what the flywheel page teaches a stranger, and in what order

Closed 2026-08-18 by the operator's plannotator round on
`sessions/2026-08-18-page-teaching-order/page-teaching-order.md` (item #274).

## The decision

The home page addresses **the stranger who has installed nothing**, and
nobody else. Its job is to show the system working, never to describe it at
reference depth — the README is the reference; the page's franchise is
showing. Operator content demotes to a link.

The page teaches **five beats, in this order**, each earning the next:

1. **What it is, in one plain sentence — above the fold.** The identity is
   **an AI-DLC harness**: roughly *"An AI-DLC harness for Claude Code:
   teams of agents on two coupled loops, design and construction, built on
   OpenSpec."* The hero plate (the rings) stays as the illustration of this
   beat; the poetic deck may ride beside the plain sentence, never instead
   of it. (Framing corrected by the operator in the round: harness + loops
   + teams + OpenSpec, not the gate.)
2. **The problem — why one conversation fails.** Design and construction
   answer to different things; fused, you design under build pressure or
   build against a moving design. Today's "Why two loops" moves up and
   shrinks.
3. **The mechanism, walked — one idea, end to end.** One raw sentence
   walked dispatch → intent → design session → decision → gate → bolt →
   merged, with the artifact each step leaves behind. The tour's subject is
   one idea's journey, not the architecture. The home page carries a
   compact walk; the #179-style tour pages go deeper.
4. **Your place in it — the gate, and how little it costs.** Annotate or
   approve in a browser; one word releases a whole batch; past the gate it
   runs without you. The differentiator, taught here — not in beat 1.
5. **Install — two commands, last.**

**What leaves the home page**: the actor table, the write-scope diagram,
the bolt state machine, "working on the flywheel itself" — operator
reference, moved behind one Overview/docs link or deferred to the README.
The home page keeps at most one architecture picture: the hero plate.

## Settled sub-questions

- **Q1 — audience**: stranger only; operator content behind a link. Yes.
- **Q2 — beat 3's home**: compact walk on the home page; tour pages go
  deeper. Yes.
- **Q3 — beat order**: problem (2) before mechanism (3). Yes.

## Consequences

1. The interactive mock-ups of the restructured site (inherited question
   #181) take the five beats as their top-level sections; nav visible
   without scrolling on beat 1's screen; current tab detail moves under
   Overview.
2. The concept-tour mock-ups (inherited question #179) are beat 3: each
   concept walks one idea end to end; architecture tours are out of brief.
3. The hero keeps the plate and gains the plain AI-DLC-harness sentence;
   the ratio cells are beat-3 material and may move down or into the walk.
