DECISION 2 · #285

The concept tour — one idea, walked end to end

Three candidate concepts, each a raw sentence a stranger might actually say. Switch concepts, then walk the seven steps; every step shows what happens and the artifact it leaves behind. The steps never change — that constancy is the lesson. Only the idea does.

The tour's form

Stepper (the mock above)

One step at a time, prev/next, rail shows where you are. The operator walks; nothing competes for attention.

+ enforces the journey's order; deep-linkable per step

− requires seven clicks to see it all

Scrollytelling

All seven steps as one tall page; the rail pins and lights up as you scroll past each artifact.

+ scroll is the native gesture; no controls to learn

− heavier to build well; skimming breaks the beat

All-visible rail

Seven columns side by side, artifacts underneath — the whole journey in one screen, nothing hidden.

+ one glance shows the shape end to end

− artifacts shrink to postage stamps; order is implied, not felt
